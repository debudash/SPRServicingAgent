# SPR Financials — SecureGrowth Fixed Annuity
## Product Summary

### What Is the SecureGrowth Fixed Annuity?

The SecureGrowth Fixed Annuity is a tax-deferred retirement savings product issued by SPR Financials. It guarantees a fixed interest rate for the full contract term, protecting your principal from market volatility. It is designed for customers who prioritize stability and predictable growth over higher-risk investment returns.

This is not an investment product. SPR Financials representatives and AI assistants are not licensed to provide personalized financial or investment advice. Customers with questions about whether this product is right for their situation should speak with a licensed financial advisor.

---

### Key Product Features

- **Guaranteed Interest Rate:** 4.25% annually, fixed for the life of the contract
- **Contract Term:** 7 years
- **Minimum Initial Deposit:** $10,000
- **Maximum Annual Contribution:** $100,000
- **Tax Deferral:** Earnings grow tax-deferred until withdrawal
- **Principal Protection:** Your initial deposit is fully protected against loss
- **Beneficiary Designation:** One or more beneficiaries may be named at account opening or updated at any time by contacting SPR Financials

---

### Eligibility

- Must be a U.S. resident aged 18 to 85 at time of application
- Valid government-issued ID required
- Social Security Number required for tax reporting

---

### Withdrawals

Customers may request a withdrawal at any time. However, withdrawals made before the end of the contract term are subject to a surrender charge (see Fee Schedule). Withdrawals are processed within 5–7 business days of a verified, confirmed request.

**Free Withdrawal Provision:** Each contract year, customers may withdraw up to 10% of their account balance without incurring a surrender charge. Amounts above 10% are subject to the applicable surrender charge for that contract year.

**Required Minimum Distributions (RMDs):** If this annuity is held inside a qualified retirement account (e.g. IRA), federal RMD rules apply starting at age 73. RMD withdrawals are exempt from surrender charges.

---

### Death Benefit

Upon the death of the account holder, the full account value (including accrued interest) is paid to the named beneficiary or beneficiaries, free of surrender charges.

---

### Renewals

At the end of the 7-year contract term, the account automatically renews for another 7-year term at the then-current guaranteed rate, unless the customer requests a full withdrawal or transfer within the 30-day renewal window.
