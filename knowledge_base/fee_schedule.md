# SPR Financials — SecureGrowth Fixed Annuity
## Fee & Surrender Charge Schedule

### Surrender Charges

A surrender charge applies to withdrawals that exceed the free withdrawal provision (10% of account balance per contract year). The charge is calculated as a percentage of the **excess withdrawal amount** — the portion above the free 10%.

| Contract Year | Surrender Charge Rate |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Year 1        | 8%                   |
| Year 2        | 7%                   |
| Year 3        | 6%                   |
| Year 4        | 5%                   |
| Year 5        | 3%                   |
| Year 6        | 1%                   |
| Year 7        | 0%                   |

> **Note:** The surrender charge drops from 5% to 3% between Year 4 and Year 5 — not a uniform 1% reduction. Customers in Year 5 often assume the charge is 4%; it is 3%.

---

### How Surrender Charges Are Calculated

**Formula:**
`Surrender Charge = (Withdrawal Amount − Free Withdrawal Amount) × Surrender Charge Rate`

**Free Withdrawal Amount:**
`Free Withdrawal Amount = Account Balance × 10%`

**Example:**
- Account balance: $50,000
- Contract year: 3
- Requested withdrawal: $20,000
- Free withdrawal amount: $50,000 × 10% = $5,000
- Excess withdrawal: $20,000 − $5,000 = $15,000
- Surrender charge rate (Year 3): 6%
- **Surrender charge: $15,000 × 6% = $900**
- **Net amount received: $20,000 − $900 = $19,100**

### IRS Early Withdrawal Penalty

Withdrawals taken before age 59½ may be subject to a 10% IRS early withdrawal penalty in addition to the surrender charge. This is a federal tax obligation, not an SPR Financials fee. Customers should consult a tax advisor for guidance on their specific situation. SPR Financials does not provide tax advice.

---

### No Surrender Charge Situations

Surrender charges are waived in the following circumstances:
- Withdrawal taken during the 30-day renewal window at end of contract term
- Death of the account holder (death benefit paid to beneficiary)
- Terminal illness diagnosis (with supporting documentation)
- Required Minimum Distribution (RMD) amounts for qualified accounts
