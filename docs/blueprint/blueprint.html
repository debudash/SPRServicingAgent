SPR Servicing Desk

A multi-agent desk for withdrawals and hardship cases. Language models read, explain and judge. Code routes every step and holds the authority to move money.

The desk never moves money unless identity, the shown quote, the customer's explicit confirmation and an independent compliance approval of that exact proposal are all established, and it never grants a waiver.

Where the authority lives

Guards are code, tested with a scripted model

Both identity gates first

No account node runs until Gate A (the record) and Gate B (this session's challenge) both pass.

FR-02 · GR-12

Quote, yes, token, then execute

The token is an HMAC over the proposal hash. Change the amount and the token is void.

FR-08 · GR-16 to GR-18

Compliance cannot override a flag

A rule flag sends the case to a person, whatever Compliance says.

FR-11

One withdrawal per proposal

An idempotency key makes a replayed execute a no-op.

FR-09

Budgets stop runaway turns

8 hops, 12 tool calls, 30 seconds per turn; $0.10 per conversation. Running out escalates.

FR-20 · D-10

Fail closed

Checker or queue down, or execution switched off: nothing moves, the customer is told what happens next.

Two sources of truth

Numbers from records, narrative from documents

desk.db (SQLite)

Accounts without restricted fields; an identity table only the gate can call; transactions, proposals, cases; an append-only audit table.

PG-01 · PG-04 · PG-10

knowledge/ → Chroma

Versioned documents with an ID, version and effective date on every chunk. Includes three synthetic SPR operations documents and, from B4, a superseded fee schedule.

PG-05 · PG-06 · FR-24

One clock, one scrubber

Contract year and age derive from the as-of date. One function strips DOB and SSN digits from anything bound for a prompt, trace, log or packet.

D-04 · FR-23

Trace and audit kept apart

LangSmith traces each conversation for debugging. The audit table answers a regulator: python -m agents.audit <conversation_id>.
