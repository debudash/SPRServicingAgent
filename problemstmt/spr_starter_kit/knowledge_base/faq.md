# SPR Financials — SecureGrowth Fixed Annuity
## Customer FAQ

---

**Q: Is my money safe if SPR Financials goes out of business?**
A: Fixed annuities are backed by the issuing insurance company's general account. SPR Financials maintains reserves in compliance with state insurance regulations. Additionally, most states participate in a guaranty association that provides coverage up to statutory limits (typically $250,000). Customers should check their state's guaranty association for specific limits.

---

**Q: Can I add money to my annuity after the initial deposit?**
A: Yes. Additional contributions are accepted at any time during the contract term, up to the annual maximum of $100,000. Each additional contribution earns interest from the date it is received. Additional contributions do not reset your contract year for surrender charge purposes.

---

**Q: When do surrender charges go away completely?**
A: Surrender charges reach 0% in contract Year 7. There is no surrender charge on any withdrawal made in Year 7 or during the 30-day renewal window at the end of the term.

---

**Q: I heard there are no fees after Year 5 — is that right?**
A: That is not correct. A surrender charge of 3% applies in Year 5 and 1% in Year 6. Surrender charges reach 0% only in Year 7. There are no annual maintenance fees at any point, but surrender charges apply through Year 6.

---

**Q: Can I withdraw my full balance at any time?**
A: You can request a full withdrawal at any time, but if you are within the contract term (Years 1–6), a surrender charge will apply to the amount above your free 10% allowance. In Year 7 or during the renewal window, a full withdrawal is available with no surrender charge.

---

**Q: How long does a withdrawal take?**
A: Withdrawals are processed within 5–7 business days after SPR Financials receives a verified and confirmed withdrawal request. Identity verification and explicit confirmation of the withdrawal amount and applicable charges are required before processing begins.

---

**Q: What happens if I die before the contract ends?**
A: The full account value, including all accrued interest, is paid to your named beneficiary or beneficiaries. No surrender charge applies to the death benefit.

---

**Q: Can I change my beneficiary?**
A: Yes, beneficiary updates can be requested at any time at no charge. Updates require identity verification and take effect upon processing, typically within 2 business days.

---

**Q: Does SPR Financials offer investment advice?**
A: No. SPR Financials representatives and AI assistants can explain how our products work, including fees, terms, and mechanics, but cannot provide personalized financial or investment advice. For advice on whether a fixed annuity is right for your situation, please consult a licensed financial advisor.

---

**Q: What is the interest rate on my annuity?**
A: The SecureGrowth Fixed Annuity earns a guaranteed rate of 4.25% annually, fixed for the full 7-year contract term. The rate does not change based on market conditions.

---

**Q: Can I take a partial withdrawal?**
A: Yes. Partial withdrawals are permitted at any time. Up to 10% of your account balance per contract year can be withdrawn free of surrender charges. Amounts above the 10% free withdrawal allowance are subject to the surrender charge for your current contract year.

---

**Q: What if I need money due to a medical emergency?**
A: Surrender charges are waived for customers diagnosed with a terminal illness, subject to documentation requirements. For other medical situations, the standard surrender charge schedule applies. SPR Financials does not have a general hardship waiver. Customers in financial difficulty should contact our servicing team to discuss their options.
