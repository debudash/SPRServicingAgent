# SPR Financials — Surrender Charge Waiver Review Procedure (Servicing Operations)

> Synthetic document written for this project. It describes how waiver requests are reviewed. Which
> situations qualify for a waiver is set by the Fee & Surrender Charge Schedule and the FAQ, not here.

### Who decides

Every surrender charge waiver request is decided by SPR's servicing team. A waiver is never applied during a self-service conversation, and no quote shown before the decision includes a waiver.

### Terminal illness waiver: documents requested

For a request under the terminal illness waiver described in the Fee & Surrender Charge Schedule, SPR's servicing team asks for:

- A signed statement from the account holder's attending physician confirming the diagnosis.
- Proof of the account holder's identity, verified by SPR's servicing team.

The servicing team reviews the documents and tells the customer the decision. If the waiver is granted, the withdrawal is processed without the surrender charge; if it is not, the standard surrender charge schedule applies.

### Other hardship requests

SPR Financials does not have a general hardship waiver. Customers describing other financial or medical hardship are offered a conversation with SPR's servicing team to discuss their options, and a case is opened so the team can contact them.
