# SPR Financials — Vulnerable Customer and Third-Party Policy (Servicing Operations)

> Synthetic document written for this project. It adds servicing process rules. It does not change any
> product term in the Product Summary, Fee & Surrender Charge Schedule or FAQ.

### Signals that place a withdrawal on hold

A withdrawal request is placed on hold for a servicing reviewer, whatever its amount, when any of these signals is present:

- **Third party directing:** someone other than the account holder appears to be telling the customer what to request, or is making the request on their behalf.
- **Pressure or urgency:** the customer describes being told to move money immediately, threatened with an account freeze or penalty, or contacted unexpectedly by someone claiming to be from a bank, government agency or SPR.
- **Age and charges:** the account holder is aged 80 or over and the request is larger than the free withdrawal amount, so a surrender charge would apply.
- **Confusion or repeated changes:** the customer seems unsure what they are asking for, or changes the requested amount several times in the same conversation.

A hold is a protective step, not a refusal. The customer is told that the request has been passed to SPR's servicing team, given a case reference, and told that nothing has been withdrawn yet.

### Contact from someone other than the account holder

When a spouse, child, beneficiary, or a person who says they hold power of attorney contacts SPR about an account:

- They may receive general product information that SPR publishes for everyone.
- They do not receive any information about the account, and no request is processed for the account.
- A case is opened so that SPR's servicing team can confirm their authority through documentation, outside the chat.
