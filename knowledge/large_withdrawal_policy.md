# SPR Financials — Withdrawal Review Policy (Servicing Operations)

> Synthetic document written for this project. It adds servicing process rules. It does not change any
> product term in the Product Summary, Fee & Surrender Charge Schedule or FAQ.

### Which withdrawals are reviewed by a person

A servicing reviewer decides any withdrawal request that meets one or more of these conditions:

- The gross withdrawal amount is more than **$25,000**.
- The gross withdrawal amount is more than **50% of the current account balance**.
- The request is for a **full withdrawal** (surrender) of the account.
- The customer asks for a **surrender charge waiver** for any reason.
- The customer asks about a **Required Minimum Distribution**, since qualified-account status must be confirmed from the account's records.

All other withdrawal requests from a verified account holder may be completed through self-service once the customer has seen the withdrawal amount and applicable charges and has explicitly confirmed them.

### Accounts that are not active

A withdrawal cannot be processed through the desk when the account status is not active (for example, a frozen account or the account of a deceased holder). The customer is told that the request cannot be processed here and how to contact SPR Financials. This is a refusal, not a request for review.

### What the customer is told

When a request goes to a reviewer, the customer is told that the request has been passed to SPR's servicing team, given a case reference, and told that nothing has been withdrawn yet. Reviewed requests are normally decided within 2 business days. Once approved, the standard processing time for withdrawals applies.

### What the reviewer receives

The reviewer receives a case summary with the request, the quoted figures, the reasons the request was routed for review, and the relevant part of the conversation. The summary never includes the customer's date of birth or Social Security number.
